# FarmDirect 🌾
## Farm to Your Doorstep in 24 Hours

FarmDirect is a modern agriculture marketplace platform designed to connect farmers directly with consumers without middlemen. The platform enables farmers to sell fresh produce at fair prices while consumers receive fresh farm products with complete transparency and faster delivery.

This project was developed as part of the **Design Thinking & Innovation Challenge** to demonstrate how digital technology can improve the agricultural supply chain and empower farmers across India.

# 📌 Problem Statement

In traditional agricultural markets, farmers often depend on multiple middlemen to sell their produce. Because of this, farmers receive lower profits while consumers pay higher prices for the same products. Small-scale farmers also face many challenges such as:

- Lack of direct market access
- Unfair pricing systems
- Limited digital knowledge
- Food wastage due to delayed transportation
- Lack of transparency in the supply chain
- Difficulty reaching customers directly

At the same time, consumers struggle to find fresh, trustworthy, and affordable farm products with proper quality assurance and transparency about where the produce comes from.

These problems create inefficiency in the agricultural ecosystem and negatively affect both farmers and consumers.

---

# 💡 Solution

FarmDirect provides a smart digital platform that directly connects farmers and consumers in a transparent and efficient way.

The platform allows farmers to:
- Upload and manage produce listings
- Receive AI-based pricing suggestions
- Manage orders and earnings
- Reach customers directly without middlemen

Consumers can:
- Browse fresh farm produce
- Order directly from verified farmers
- Track orders live
- Subscribe to weekly fresh baskets
- Chat with farmers for transparency and trust

FarmDirect aims to:
- Increase farmer profits
- Reduce unnecessary middlemen
- Deliver fresher products faster
- Reduce food wastage
- Build trust through transparency
- Promote sustainable agriculture practices
