How It Works

From Soil to Table in Three Simple Steps

No middlemen. No cold storage surprises. Just fresh produce going directly from the farm to your kitchen.

STEP 01
🌾

Farmer Lists Produce

Farmers upload fresh crops using voice notes, photos, or text in their regional language. AI suggests the best price. Live in 90 seconds.

STEP 02
🔍

You Browse & Order

Filter by location, crop, price, and organic status. See the farmer's face, village, and ratings. Order with one tap.

STEP 03
📦

Packed & Delivered

Fresh from the farm, packed with a QR code, and delivered to your door. Live tracking every step of the way.

STEP 04
⭐

Rate & Build Trust

Upload a delivery photo, rate freshness and accuracy, earn FarmCoins. Your review helps other buyers and rewards honest farmers.
